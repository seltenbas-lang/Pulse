## What Pulse does and doesn't do

- Pulse is a player. It does not host, upload, sell or share any music. Songs are played from publicly
  available sources on the listener's own device, and nothing is sent to the developers.
- There are no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no payments. Nobody earns money from Pulse.
- Lyrics come from a public lyrics source (LRCLIB); covers and song details from the services that
  publish them.
